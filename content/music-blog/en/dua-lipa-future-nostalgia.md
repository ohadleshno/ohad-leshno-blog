So "normal" as Dua Lipa. However, if I go into the depth of her music, then I find that there is another layer to it that really produces something pleasant and fun. Future Nostalgia is an album that is a combination of 80's pop music and futuristic music, hence the name Future Nostalgia. Lupe described it as a "nostalgic pop album that feels like a dance lesson" She drew her inspiration from the music of the 80's to create a sound that feels familiar and innovative in equal measure.

The songs Don't Start Now, Physical and Break My Heart are all very worthy singles, with catchy melodies that will stick in your head for weeks after listening. The synthesizer found in many of the Future Nostalgia songs is the key to bringing back the 80's vibe. Other songs such as Levitating, Hallucinate, Pretty Please, Cool and Good in Bed all also have exciting melodies that will make you dance, and will still sound "new" in some ways, even after multiple listens. The songs I have described here collectively describe the aesthetic and emotional side of love:

<blockquote dir="auto">
"Who needs to go to sleep when I got you next to me?"
</blockquote>

And on the other hand the destructive nature of love.

<blockquote dir="auto">
"You broke my heart / And now I'm picking up the pieces"
</blockquote>

In addition, the pop ballad Boys Will Be Boys powerfully examines the issue of female empowerment, in a time of toxic masculinity and ongoing harassment in our modern society.

<blockquote dir="auto">
“It’s second nature to walk home before the sun goes down / And put your keys between your knuckles when there’s boys around,”
</blockquote>

Her point is clear, girls have to grow up much faster than their male friends, who can mostly remain unaware of the world's violence until a later age, while their female friends burst their childhood bubbles with self-defense advice.

I don't think it's a great song musically, but I appreciate her attempt to convey a meaningful message. It's important to hear a song that talks about important issues and not just about sex, money and cars.

The album Future Nostalgia was released on March 27, 2020, and a few days later the world went into lockdown due to the Corona epidemic. Ironically enough, the goal of the album was to create music that provides escapism for the world's problems. Dua Lipa said that she wanted to make an album that would make people dance and forget about their troubles for a while.

<blockquote dir="auto">
“I wanted to make music that takes your mind away from that. I wanted to just make it a bit easier for me to get out of bed and not think about the negative things that are going on in the world all the time.”
</blockquote>

As part of the launch of her new album, Dua Lipa appeared at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4neLJQC1_E&ab_channel=NPRMusic" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 underline hover:text-indigo-500 font-medium break-all">Tiny Desk Concert</a>, not opened but because at the same time There was a time when the world was shut down, she had to perform in another studio. I think her live versions are more interesting than her original versions, because they allow her to express herself in a more personal way; This is particularly evident in the song Love Again. I personally heard this performance maybe dozens of times.

The album Future Nostalgia is an album that does exactly what an album in its style is supposed to do: fun, poppy, addictive and memorable. He kind of sets the trend for a (almost) new category of trend music.

I highly recommend the album to anyone who likes pop. This is a great album, which is sure to warm your heart. This entire post is dedicated to Saar Yaakov Panani, the legend in his life who happens to have a birthday today. (full of happiness, full of love)

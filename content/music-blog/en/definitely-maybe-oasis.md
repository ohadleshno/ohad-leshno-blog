The album "Definitely Maybe" landed right at a critical time for the British music scene, offering a refreshing alternative to the grunge movement that dominated at the time. For those unfamiliar, grunge music generally consists of a "dirty" guitar sound, strong riffs and heavy drumming. The "dirty" sound was achieved as a result of a change in the traditional playing style of punk rock, and with a lot of use of distortion.  Oasis, led by brothers Liam and Noel Gallagher, combined their music with a unique blend of rock and pop, creating a new sound that spoke to an entire generation. This album came to the world right after the end of Nirvana that the world was just waiting for something new to come.

The album is characterized by bold guitar riffs, direct drum beats and Liam Gallagher's unique vocals. Songs like "Rock 'n' Roll Star" and "Cigarettes & Alcohol" demonstrate the band's blunt and unapologetic approach to rock music. Noel Gallagher's songwriting ability stands out, combining catchy melodies with mournful lyrics, and striking a balance between arrogance and vulnerability. And that's what works so well in the album, the terrible vulnerability in the words and singing against the very strong, fast and penetrating rhythm of the songs. Really a couple of rascals who did a lot of drugs but understood the human soul and managed to make them sing for their girlfriends for years wonderwall (full disclosure, I love).

One of the defining features of the album is its immediacy. Songs like "Live forever" and "Supersonic" break out with an infectious energy that cannot be ignored. The lyrics, written by Noel Gallagher, are a mixture of smug arrogance and honest introspection, capturing the perceptions, dreams and frustrations of working-class youth in 1990s Britain.

Even decades after its release, the album continues to touch, with timeless themes of youth, rebellion and longing that transcend the boundaries of time and space. Whether you're a die-hard fan or a curious newcomer, this album remains a must-listen for anyone who loves honest, unapologetic rock and roll. The album is a cultural landmark that continues to inspire and captivate audiences with its electrifying blend of melody, attitude and passion. Oasis may have come and gone, but their music lives on, forever etched in the annals of rock history.

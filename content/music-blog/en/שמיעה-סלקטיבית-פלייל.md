I am probably not unique in the experience I am about to describe: you put on headphones leaving home, open your music app, and reach the point of deciding what song to play. The last thing you want at that moment is thinking about selecting a song: you simply want music that plays smoothly, fun, easy listening. In most cases that doesn't happen, and in the best case you get stuck on the same tired Spotify playlist you've overplayed. In the worst case you start inventing customs like pretending music is forbidden during mourning periods.

So especially for you, I present the first playlist of the Selective Hearing blog, designed to provide music for people while they pretend to work.

Hopefully it will let you enjoy pleasant music without overthinking.

Enjoy the music.

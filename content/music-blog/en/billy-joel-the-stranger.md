Today we will talk about Billy Joel, who is one of my favorite artists. His songs, such as "Piano Man", "Uptown Girl", "Just the Way You Are" and "New York State of Mind", are all very well known. They are so popular that the chances that you haven't been exposed to them are slim. I know that some people may think that it is a bit basic to love a classic pop singer, that you can easily see him as the countryman of the United States, that instead of performing in Caesarea, he performs in Madison Square Garden. But I think he is a very interesting craftsman, and with so many garnets. Although Joel's songs have a classic pop structure, they are extremely complex and multi-layered. They are full of small details that deepen their meaning.

I usually choose from each artist an album that presents him in the most accurate way in my eyes, but in the current case I have a hard time deciding between two albums. One is Billy Joel's first album, and the other is his best known album - The Stranger. Finally, after much deliberation, I chose The Stranger album, because it is indeed the highlight of his creation. The Stranger is Billy Joel's sixth studio album, released in 1977. The album sold over ten million copies, and is one of the best selling albums of all time. The album showcases Joel's versatility, his storytelling abilities, and his undisputed musical talent. From beginning to end he fascinates the listeners with his passionate words, memorable melodies and his creativity.

The album opens with the iconic song "Movin' Out (Anthony's Song)", a high-energy song that indicates what is to come. The catchy piano pieces and lyrics about the dreams of the working class immediately grab the listener's attention. Joel's dynamic vocal performance manages to convey the frustration and determination of the song's protagonist.

Joel's lyrics in the entire album touch on a variety of topics - love, relationships, and the struggles in everyday life. His ability to tell stories stands out in songs such as "Scenes from an Italian Restaurant", a song that consists of mini-epics, which takes the listener on a nostalgic journey through different stages of a relationship. The transitions between the three distinct parts of the song are smooth, demonstrating Joel's ability to weave compelling narratives into his music.

Another example of his tremendous ability is the song "Vienna". Joel is also an excellent songwriter. He is able to express complex feelings in simple and fluent words. Through the mesmerizing melody and introspective lyrics, the song reminds listeners to slow down, embrace life's journey and find balance. The melancholic piano in the opening sets the contemplative tone, while Joel's poetry conveys a strong message about the essential slowing down in our lives. The metaphorical lyrics encourage patience and satisfaction, and remind us to appreciate the present and find fulfillment at our own pace. This important message is expressed in penetrating sentences:

<blockquote dir="auto">
"You've got your passion, you’ve got your pride. But don't you know that only fools are satisfied?"
</blockquote>

My first exposure to Billy Joel is quite awkward. I met him through watching the series Glee. Glee is a high school drama about a school choir preparing to compete in the district competition. In the series, the characters perform remakes of popular songs. In one of the episodes, the character Kurt Pagan performed the song "Only the Good Die Young", and ironically, the actor who played Kurt, Corey Montetio, passed away in 2015.

I discovered the fact that Bailey Goel is Jewish by the way: the character in the series, Korban, said in the same scene that he continues in a sequence of singing only songs by Jewish singers. After a short inquiry I found out that Billy Goel is indeed Jewish, and that he grew up in Brooklyn. So here too the Jewish connection is strong and preserved.

The song is a controversial song, which combines elements of rock and gospel, and its revolutionary lyrics caused a stir when they came out. Joel fearlessly approaches the subject of religion, presenting a thought-provoking perspective. The energy of the song is infectious, which almost forces the listener to join in the singing.

The album concludes with the song "Everybody Has a Dream", a touching ballad that explores the universal human experience of aspiration and hope for the future. Joel's introspective lyrics remind the listener that dreams are an essential part of our humanity, and the gradual intensity of the song, accompanied by a rich accompaniment, adds depth to the composition. The song serves as a beautiful conclusion to the album, which brings together themes such as love, self-discovery, and the importance of sticking to your dreams despite the difficulties.

Musically, "The Stranger" is a masterpiece in songwriting and design. Joel's piano skills stand out at the forefront of his work, but the album also incorporates elements of rock, pop, jazz and even Latin influences. In my opinion, Billy Joel is one of the most important and influential artists in modern music. He managed to create music that is loved by people all over the world.

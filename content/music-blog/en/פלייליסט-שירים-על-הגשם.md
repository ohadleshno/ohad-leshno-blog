This time I decided that instead of an album, I would choose a motif that I started paying more attention to recently that appears in a lot of songs that I like. The motif we will wash with today is none other than the rain. I won't start writing you a lot of fancy texts about the rain, if that's what you're looking for, just go to chatgpt. Today I will try to bring the rain in its emotional and pure form and as always through the music, so instead of confusing the expert let's just start getting wet and see the aspects of the rain:

The first song in the playlist is the song from which the thought of making this playlist arose. So we will begin

In June Rechter - "and rain falls". I believe I am not the only one who felt alone, lonely and stuck. Yoni as in the sixteenth lamb and in other places just knows how to create the exact atmosphere with the words so poignant. This song is so sharp and amazing that it just washes over you. It is from the album that I wrote about before on the Tzuvah blog.

And if we're talking about the Grateful Dead, we can't help but mention Robert Zimmerman (Bob Dylan among them), who brings us another aspect of the rain with one of the greatest protest songs of all time, A Hard Rain's a-Gonna Fall, which will become the soundtrack of the civil rights movement and the new voice of the generation. (Yes, there is also a version by Aviv Gefen of the song in Hebrew)

And when I think of songs about rain, I always think, Have you ever seen the rain? Of course the rain in this context refers to depression in the middle of a Sunday day. The song was written right while cuddling CCR (maybe we'll win and write a post about their album as well).

There is one more song that I want to address before I finish, which I think will be the most surprising song in the rain over me playlist. His goal is to remember that music can be silly and fun. It reminds me of my time in high school, it is linked to the times when I went to see Fast and Furious 5 in the Rav Chen cinema.

This playlist will be signed by the song "Flood" by Keren Pels. Like my life itself, which is full of cynicism and surprises and endless breaking of character, here too I had to finish the playlist as usual with humor and good taste. The song I chose for this ending is my personal victory song, and those who know me well enough know that I used to know all the words to the song.

So, because I'm not that simple person who only knows how to listen to one style of music, I put inside a lot of good anecdotes from many worlds into the playlist from many different and varied worlds. This playlist (and I believe the following ones as well) was put together by searching the music library I had saved up. Every song there is a story and a complete experience in itself in my world. The rain is a certain dimension that simply allows the creators to relate to it and create for us an experience in front of it that will feel subjective enough to us. So many forms of rain, just let your heart open and you will discover a whole world.

Once I spoke to my brother about the music blog, and he told me about a podcast he listened to regarding two Brazilian bloggers who wrote about music in Portuguese. One day, the two decided to switch the language of the blog to Spanish. The host of the podcast asked them: "Weren't you afraid of losing the audience you already built?" Their answer was: "No, nobody really reads the blog, most are just looking for music links." This blog is not intended to be just music links, but on the other hand, as Yoni Rechter said: "words are only a substitute for what cannot be said." Or as people like to say in art, less is more, and there are things better heard and experienced yourself. Therefore, there will also be brief recommendations with concise summaries of interesting, sweet, intriguing things, mostly ones I loved and thought you would want to experience too.

My recommendations for the week, without further ado:

MGMT released a great new album called Loss Of Life: very morbid, tough atmosphere. An album that on first listen does not go down easily, but on third listen takes all your conscience down with it (the star student badge is awarded to the track "Nothing to Declare").

I listen to music on vinyl, and part of the meaning of the experience is bringing physical reality to the realm of the spirit: searching for music in its physical dimension by wandering through record stores. A record I listened to this week and loved deeply is a blast from the past: Frank Sinatra's album "A Man Alone", a true masterpiece. The transitional passages are interesting and poignant, and the songs touch the soul deeply. The song "Love's Been Good to Me" stood out for me in particular. It was like a magnifying glass on my personal experience, and I saw someone write something stunning that describes that exact experience:

<blockquote dir="auto">
"The best people in my life are those who articulate for me things my heart already felt! Sometimes when words are found for me, I call it returning a lost object."
</blockquote>

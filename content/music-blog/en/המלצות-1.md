I once talked to my brother about the music blog and he told me about some podcast he heard about a couple of Brazilian bloggers who wrote about music in Portuguese. One day, the two decided to change the language of the blog to Spanish. The podcast host asked them: "Were you not afraid of losing the audience you had already gained?". Their answer was: "No, no one really reads the blog, most of them are just looking for links to music." This blog is not meant to be just links to music, but on the other hand, as Yoni Rechter said, "Words are only a substitute for what cannot be said." Or like they like to say a lot in the art of less is more, and some things are better to hear and experience for yourself. Therefore, there will also be short recommendations here with a concise overview of interesting, cute, intriguing things (insert a non-generic descriptive word here) - mainly those that I liked and thought you would also like to experience.

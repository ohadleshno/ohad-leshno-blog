I had a big dilemma which Clapton album to choose; The truth is, I even wrote a whole post about his live album. There is something about live albums that I really like in general, and personally I think this is one of the best live albums out there. Even so, the album naturally consists of a larger amount of hits, so I feel that if we stay there - we are missing a big part of Clapton. This is why we will talk about another album which is one of Clapton's more significant works.

So before we start delving deeper into the Layla and Other Assorted Love Songs album, let's mention the composition of the album. The album is by Derek and The Dominos, formed by Clapton after he left Cream. As we know, Harrison and Clapton were very good friends and there were many collaborations between them, and he even participated in the recording of While my guitar gently sweeps away (the story behind the song is also quite special). All the members of the band worked with George Harrison on the album All Things Must Pass, and from the sessions on Harrison's album the band was built. The band didn't get to exist for long, but the combination between them worked great.

The album opens with the song I Looked Away. Apparently, it sounds like the rhythm in the song is going to be calm, but as the song progresses, the rhythm increases like the emotional process of the protagonist in the song, a kind of digestion of the breakup he went through.

<blockquote dir="auto">
"She took my hand
And tried to make me understand
That she would always be there
But I looked away
And she ran away from me today
I'm such a lonely man
</blockquote>

The song ends with a guitar solo that introduces us to the high tempo of the album. This process, which has a slow start and progress throughout the song, is repeated many times on the album: in Bell Bottom Blues - and in other songs such as in the cover of the blues song Have You Ever Loved a Women and in It's To Late, another song that talks about lost love.

Guitar battles are a very important part of the album and especially in the song Anyday. This song is Clapton's guitar battle with Duane Allman (member of The Allman Brothers). The guitars scream what Clapton's heart desires

<blockquote dir="auto">
"And I know anyday, anyday I will see you smile, I will see you smile"
</blockquote>

Clapton is an artist who succeeds with the variety of tools at his disposal, to convey his being. The guitar here in the song is another way of communication to reach us, the listeners, so that we understand his desire for love.

The highlights of the album, for me, are two songs that are a kind of jam that became a song. Why does love got to be so sad is a song in which the energy appears from the first second, the guitar solo is amazing, and there is nothing to say in words - go listen. And the second song, Keep on Growing, which was originally a jam by the band, and they even thought of removing it from the album until Bobby Whitlock insisted that it was too good, and credited the many with this amazing song.

Finally, we must talk about the song Layla.  Perhaps the best known song in the history of rock. The story behind it is a story about a love that failed to come to fruition. Layla היא דמות מפואמה של משורר פרסי בשם ניזאמי, שעסקה בסיפור אהבה טרגי, וקלפטון השתמש בסיפור הזה כמקור השראה לשיר. במהלך התקופה שקלפטון עבד עם ג'ורג' האריסון הוא התאהב בפאטי בויד אשתו של ג'ורג', וכתב את השיר עליה. Clapton wrote the song thinking the message would convince Boyd to leave Harrison for him. Indeed, Boyd divorced George and married Clapton ("I would also divorce with a song like that"). אבל כמובן כמו כל כוכב רוק עם אורח חיים לא מסודר, קפריזות של ילד בן שמונה והתמכרות לאלכוהול וסמים, הם התגרשו אחרי עשור.

So much has been written and there is much more to expand on this album which is all intertwined with complex history, great love and amazing music. The album is a mix of calm blues, fearsome guitar battles and powerful feelings towards the loves of life, as only Clapton knows.

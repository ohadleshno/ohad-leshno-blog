I'm sure I'm not unique in the experience I'm going to describe right now, you put on headphones when you leave the house and you go into your music application and you've reached the point where you have to decide which song you want to hear. The last thing you want at that point is to think about which song to choose, you just want music that will play in your ear and be "flowing", "fun", "something uplifting bro, put on something uplifting". In most cases, this is not what happens, and in the end, at best, you get stuck on the same crappy Spotify playlist that you've already plowed through too much. In the worst case, you start inventing customs such as not listening to music during the count of the Omer.

So especially for you I present the first playlist of the Selective Hearing blog which aims to provide people with music while they make themselves work.

Hopefully he will make you listen to music that is nice without thinking and without making you shoot yourself in the head.
